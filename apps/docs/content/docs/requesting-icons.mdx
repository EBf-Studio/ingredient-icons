New icons ship in batches. To ask for one, [open an issue](https://github.com/EBf-Studio/ingredient-icons/issues/new) with:

- the **catalog id** of the ingredient (`basmati-rice`), or its name in any language if it isn't in the catalog or you don't know the id;
- optionally, **what it looks like** where you cook: a photo link helps for regional ingredients.

Before asking, check that the ingredient doesn't already get a good icon through its parents: search the [gallery](/icons), which also finds ingredients by the icon they borrow, or try it in the demo on the [home page](/).

## Ingredients missing from the catalog

Icons are keyed by catalog id, so an ingredient the catalog doesn't know is added to it first, with its parent, its family and its names. Marmalade maintains the catalog. Once the ingredient is in, it gets the icon of its parent right away, and its own when one is drawn.

## What gets drawn first

Ingredients that show up in many recipes, and ones whose fallback is misleading. Basmati showing the rice icon is fine for a while. An ingredient that falls back to the generic icon, or to something that looks wrong, comes first.

## Reporting a problem

An icon that looks wrong, a misleading fallback, a wrong parent or a bad name: open an issue with the id and what you expected. Parents and names come from the catalog, so a fix there reaches every app with the next release.
