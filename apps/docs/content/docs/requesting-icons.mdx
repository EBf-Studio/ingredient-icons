New icons ship in batches. To ask for one, [open an issue](https://github.com/EBf-Studio/ingredient-icons/issues/new) with:

- the **Open Food Facts id** of the ingredient (`en:yuzu`), or its name if you don't know the id;
- optionally, **what it looks like** where you cook: a photo link helps for regional ingredients.

Before asking, check that the ingredient doesn't already get a good icon through its parents: search the [gallery](/icons), which also finds ingredients by the icon they borrow.

## What gets drawn first

Ingredients that show up in many recipes, and ones whose fallback is misleading. Lardons showing bacon is fine for a while. An ingredient that falls back to nothing, like yuzu today, or to something that looks wrong, comes first.

## Reporting a problem

An icon that looks wrong, a misleading fallback or a bad translation: open an issue with the id and what you expected. Fallbacks come from the Open Food Facts taxonomy, so some fixes belong [upstream](https://github.com/openfoodfacts/openfoodfacts-server).
