Ingredient Icons gives every ingredient of a recipe its own picture. There are <Total /> icons today, and each one is named after an id of the [Open Food Facts ingredients taxonomy](https://wiki.openfoodfacts.org/Global_taxonomies), so a recipe that stores `en:shallot` already knows which icon to show.

<IconRow ids={["en:shallot", "en:fresh-cream", "en:salt", "en:black-pepper", "en:brie", "en:basil", "en:lemon", "en:salmon"]} />

## Why not emojis?

Emojis run out fast in a kitchen. There is no emoji for a shallot or fresh cream, and 🧂 has to stand for both salt and pepper. Recipe apps end up with half their ingredients drawn as a generic bowl.

## What you get

- **<Total /> icons**, in one consistent soft 3D style, on transparent backgrounds.
- **Fallback through the taxonomy.** An ingredient without an icon gets its closest ancestor's: cherry tomatoes get the tomato, lardons get bacon. That covers <Resolvable /> taxonomy ids.
- **WebP in 64, 128 and 256 px** in the npm package, served from jsDelivr or from your own server. **PNG up to 512 px** in the repository.
- **English and French names** for every icon, from Open Food Facts.
- **Public domain.** The icons are CC0: no attribution, no license file to ship.
