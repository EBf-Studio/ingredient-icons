Ingredient Icons gives every ingredient of a recipe its own picture. There are <Total /> icons today, and each one is named after an id of [Marmalade](https://try-marmalade.com)'s ingredient catalog, so a recipe that stores `shallot` already knows which icon to show. The package ships the catalog too: `catalog.json`, with <CatalogSize /> ingredients and families, their parents and their names in six languages.

<IconRow ids={["shallot", "creme-fraiche", "salt", "black-pepper", "brie", "basil", "lemon", "salmon"]} />

## Why not emojis?

Emojis run out fast in a kitchen. There is no emoji for a shallot or crème fraîche, and 🧂 has to stand for both salt and pepper. Recipe apps end up with half their ingredients drawn as a generic bowl.

## What you get

- **<Total /> icons**, in one consistent soft 3D style, on transparent backgrounds.
- **Fallback through the catalog.** Every ingredient has one parent, up to one of <FamilyCount /> families. An ingredient without an icon gets its nearest ancestor's: extra virgin olive oil gets the olive oil, a brioche bun gets the bread. That covers <Covered /> of the <CatalogSize /> catalog entries.
- **WebP in 64, 128 and 256 px** in the npm package, served from jsDelivr or from your own server. **PNG up to 512 px** in the repository.
- **Names in six languages** in `catalog.json`: French and English for every entry, Spanish, Italian, German and Portuguese where Wikidata has them.
- **Public domain.** The icons and the catalog are CC0: no attribution, no license file to ship.
